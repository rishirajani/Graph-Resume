    Applications

    Jobs you've applied for, with the tailored link you sent and how many people opened it. Kept in applications.json in your private repository; never published.

    Tailored links

    Tailored links you've made for specific jobs. Switching one off or letting it run out takes it off your site within a few minutes; the link then shows your public resume instead. In GoatCounter each link appears by its code, e.g. #for/k3x9ab.
