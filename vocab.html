Graph Resume vocabulary

The gr: terms behind the resume graph: what they mean and how they connect.

A small vocabulary for describing a person's career as a knowledge graph: the roles they held, what they studied and built, the skills each of those used, and how the pieces connect. It builds on schema.org for the kinds of things (people, organisations, credentials, creative works, defined terms) and adds the relations between them that schema.org doesn't have, or only has as plain text.

How it fits together

The kinds of things come from schema.org: the person is a schema:Person, each job a schema:OrganizationRole, each degree or certificate a schema:EducationalOccupationalCredential, each project a schema:CreativeWork, each skill a schema:DefinedTerm grouped into skill areas (schema:DefinedTermSet). Only awards and career breaks needed classes of their own.

The relations are gr: terms, because schema.org mostly links a person straight to an organisation, or describes skills as plain text. Here every job, degree and project is its own node, linked to where it happened and to the skills it used, so each skill on the resume can be traced back to the work behind it. Where a gr: relation is a special case of a schema.org property, it says so (rdfs:subPropertyOf, owl:inverseOf); where it's only similar, it points to it under “Compare”.

Skills and languages are matched to the same concepts in Wikidata and the EU's ESCO classification with skos:exactMatch and skos:closeMatch. Before each publish, the resume is checked against this vocabulary (every relation connects the kinds of things it should) and against SHACL shapes.

A role, where it was, and a skill it used, as they appear in resume.ttl:

@prefix cv: <https://rishirajani.github.io/Graph-Resume/#> .
@prefix gr: <https://rishirajani.github.io/Graph-Resume/vocab#> .
@prefix schema: <https://schema.org/> .
@prefix skos: <http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#> .

cv:me gr:workedAs cv:r-nobel .

cv:o-nobel a schema:Organization ;
    schema:name "Nobel Hygiene Pvt. Ltd." .

cv:r-nobel a schema:OrganizationRole ;
    gr:at cv:o-nobel ;
    gr:used cv:s-python ;
    schema:endDate "2024-03" ;
    schema:name "Data Engineering Consultant" ;
    schema:startDate "2023-10" .

cv:s-python a schema:DefinedTerm ;
    skos:exactMatch <http://data.europa.eu/esco/skill/ccd0a1d9-afda-43d9-b901-96344886e14d>,
        <http://www.wikidata.org/entity/Q28865> ;
    schema:name "Python" .
